How to connect a new device:
  • iPhone / iPad (Wireless RSD): Ensure device is on the Tailnet (or Wi-Fi), and pair once over USB with: pymobiledevice3 lockdown remotepairing --pair. The sidey-wireless-tunnel service connects automatically.
  • Apple TV (tvOS): Ensure Apple TV is powered on and connected to the Tailnet. Sidey discovers it over the network.
